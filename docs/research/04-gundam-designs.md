# Mobile Suit Design Archetypes — Gundam Franchise Catalog

Research date: 2026-09-02. Purpose: a reference for an ORIGINAL mech game. Nothing here should be copied literally; the value is in silhouettes, roles, weapon families, gimmicks, color logic, and story/progression tropes.

Sourcing note: in this environment the wiki hosts (gundam.fandom.com, mahq.net, wikipedia) were reachable only via search snippets, not full-page fetches. URLs below point to the canonical pages surfaced by those searches; details were cross-checked against the snippets and general franchise knowledge.

## 0. Design-language primer (the vocabulary everything else uses)

| Design family | Head / sensor | Body cues | Typical colors | Narrative meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gundam-type** (Federation hero) | Twin "eyes" (dual camera) + V-shaped forehead antenna ("V-fin"), face "mask" with chin vents, red jewel/forehead sensor | Squared chest with intake vents, slim waist, articulated skirt armor, boxy but heroic proportions, exposed backpack thrusters, "samurai helmet" head | Tricolor: white base, blue torso, red chest/feet, yellow accents (V-fin, vents) | Prototype / one-off / protagonist. Symbol of "the best machine, hand-built, too expensive to mass-produce" |
| **GM-type** (Federation grunt) | Single wide visor replacing eyes, no V-fin | Same body as Gundam but simplified: fewer vents, plainer feet, cheaper alloy | Muted red/white, later olive/grey/blue | "Mass-produced copy of the hero," cost-effective, disposable; a visor instantly reads as "grunt" |
| **Zeon-type / mono-eye** (antagonist) | Single glowing round eye on a horizontal rail, can slide/rotate; often a helmet "crest" or horn for commanders | Rounded, organic, bulging chest and shoulders; spiked shoulder pauldron on one side and a shield on the other; external power cables/tubing on waist, legs, and head; big skirt armor; tank-like feet | Olive green grunt; commanders get custom colors (Char red, Black Tri-Stars black/purple, Johnny Ridden crimson) | "Monster / cyclops," psychological warfare; villain grunt; ace customs signalled by color and horn |
| **Heavy Zeon** (Dom / Big Zam) | Mono-eye in a broad slit | Enormous skirt, bell-bottom legs housing hover jets, hunched, wide | Purple/black (Dom), green-blue (Big Zam) | Weight, elite squads, overwhelming force |
| **Amphibious Zeon** | Mono-eye in a dome or slit, no neck | Rounded hull, clawed arms, no visible head, big flat feet, hydrojets | Teal/blue/pink | Monster-movie creatures, ambush |
| **Titans / late-Federation** | Slit visor or twin eyes with heavy brow, "helmet" shapes | Angular, muscular, often asymmetric, bulk in arms | Dark blue/black, grey | Authoritarian elite; "the good guys' tech in evil hands" |
| **Neo Zeon / Newtype** | Mono-eye or hidden eyes, sweeping horns | Wide flaring binders, cape-like wings, hollow torsos with funnel racks, elegant curves | Purple/pink/white (Qubeley), red (Sazabi), green (Kshatriya) | Psychic pilot, remote weapons, aristocracy |
| **Gundam-Frame (IBO)** | Twin eyes, V-fin, but "skeletal" jaw | Exposed inner frame at joints, thick industrial armor slabs, thrusters clustered, no beam glow | Grey/white with a single accent color (Barbatos blue, Bael white/gold) | Ancient war relics, brutal melee, machines that eat their pilots |
| **WfM school-suit** | Twin eyes with "shell unit" glowing panels | Slim, angular, uniform-like paneling, shoulder "epaulettes" | White/blue with glowing red/blue permet lines | Corporate/school duel culture; drone control |

## 4. Weapon categories (with franchise examples)

**Ranged — beam**
- Beam rifle (RX-78 → everything): mid-power hitscan; E-cap ammo limit in early UC.
- Beam spray gun (GM): short-range, weak, cheap.
- Beam pistol / beam gun (Rick Dias, Dynames): fast, low damage.
- Long-range beam rifle / sniper (Dynames GN Sniper, Pharact, Gelgoog Jäger).
- Beam magnum (Unicorn): 4× rifle power, burns the barrel/arm; limited shots.
- Mega beam launcher / Hyper Mega Launcher (Zeta): shoulder-carried ship-killer.
- Mega Bazooka Launcher (Hyaku Shiki): needs an external power hookup.
- Mega particle cannon (chest/abdomen: Sazabi, Psyco Gundam, Big Zam): fixed-body big beams; diffusion versions spread.
- High Mega Cannon (ZZ head): forehead ship-killer.
- VSBR (F91): variable speed = choose pierce vs impact.
- Buster Rifle / Twin Buster Rifle (Wing): colony-scale, limited cartridges.
- Satellite Cannon (Gundam X): orbital-power charge-up "ultimate".
- GN weapons (00): particle beams; Trans-Am multiplies.
- Plasma cannons / multi-phase cannon (Freedom, Aegis): fold-out heavy beams.
- Beam launcher / hyper-impulse (Agni): pack-mounted artillery.

- Machine gun (Zaku 120mm), smoothbore cannon (Barbatos 300mm, Tieren 200mm): reliable vs beam-immune armor.

## 6. Design takeaways for the game

### 6a. Eighteen original-but-inspired FRAME archetypes

Each: silhouette, role, stat lean (Armor / Mobility / Firepower / Melee / Sensors / Energy), transform mode, signature gimmick. Names are placeholders.

1. **VANGUARD frame** (Gundam-type hero) — Boxy-heroic humanoid, twin "eyes," forked crest, chest intakes, backpack with two blade hilts, shield with emblem. Role: balanced all-rounder / protagonist prototype. Stat lean: even, slight Energy+. Transform: none (a docking "core craft" ejects as the cockpit). Gimmick: *Learning core* — frame gains a small permanent bonus per boss defeated; too expensive to mass-produce, so its "production copy" (see #2) is always weaker.
2. **LINE frame** (GM-type grunt) — Same body as #1 with a single visor strip and no crest, plainer feet, cheaper alloy. Role: cheap all-rounder for the player's allied squad / early enemies. Stat lean: Armor-, Firepower-. Transform: none. Gimmick: *Modular kit*: any weapon fits; only frame that can be repaired for free.
3. **CYCLOPS frame** (Zeon grunt) — Rounded chest, one spiked pauldron + one shield, glowing mono-eye that visibly tracks the player, external cables, tank feet, hand-axe. Role: enemy backbone; also unlockable "captured" chassis. Stat lean: Armor+, Sensors-. Transform: none. Gimmick: *Commander horn* variant = +30% thrust, custom color, and an "ace" AI that leads squads; "the red one" trope.
4. **SKATER frame** (Dom / hover-heavy) — Bell-bottom legs, huge skirt, hunched torso, mono-eye slit. Role: heavy ground assault. Stat lean: Armor++, Firepower+ (big bazooka), Mobility neutral but *only while hovering*. Transform: **hovertank** (kneels into a skirt-down hover slide with a fixed forward cannon). Gimmick: *Formation strike* — three of them dash in a line for an unblockable combo.
5. **DELTA frame** (Zeta / Wave Rider) — Long forearm shields, flying-armor wing plates, tail stabilizer, sharp angles. Role: high-mobility interceptor, re-entry capable. Stat lean: Mobility++, Armor-. Transform: **jet (flat delta glider)** that can carry an ally and ram. Gimmick: *Emotion barrier* — a short-lived glow shield that triggers when HP is critical.
6. **SAUCER frame** (Asshimar / disc) — Round head, wide chest, rotund; folds into a disc. Role: atmospheric patrol / air superiority for enemies. Stat lean: Mobility+, Sensors+, Melee-. Transform: **disc/saucer** with fixed underslung rifle. Gimmick: *Endless loiter* — no fuel limit in disc mode.
7. **TRIAD frame** (ZZ / V / Impulse combining) — Chunky, big backpack; splits into three craft. Role: swap-in-the-field heavy. Stat lean: Firepower++, Armor+, Mobility-. Transform: **three vehicles** (nose fighter, upper gunship, lower tank-legs) that re-dock; a drop-ship can swap the upper/lower halves mid-mission (mobility / sword / artillery halves). Gimmick: *Head cannon* ship-killer with a long charge; *Boots strike* — kick the leg module off as a bomb.
8. **SEER frame** (Qubeley / Nu / Kshatriya funnel-caster) — Slender body, flared shoulder binders or a single asymmetric "wing" of stacked drone fins. Role: Newtype remote-weapons specialist. Stat lean: Sensors++, Energy+, Armor-. Transform: none. Gimmick: *Drone fins* — 6–24 detachable bits that orbit, snipe, or lock into a tetrahedral barrier; only usable after the pilot's "sense" stat is unlocked; a higher-tier version can hijack enemy drones (NT-D).
9. **CRIMSON frame** (Sazabi / Sinanju ace-heavy) — Massive rounded shoulders, cape-like backpack, mono-eye, gold emblem. Role: rival boss / late unlock. ### 6c. "Prototype → Production → Ace custom" tiering

Use it both for the player's progression and for how enemy rosters read on-screen:

| Tier | Visual language | Stats | How the player gets it | Example lineage (franchise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T0 Trainer** | Visor, plain, stubby, single accent color | Baseline; free repairs | Starting unit / school | Demi Trainer, Genoace, Leo, Ball |
| **T1 Production grunt** | Visor or mono-eye, no crest, faction color | 100% baseline; cheap parts | Bought in bulk; squadmates | GM, Zaku II, Graze, Tieren, Dagger, GINN |
| **T2 Ace custom** | Same chassis + commander horn/fin, custom paint (red, black, gold), asymmetric addition (one big gun, gatling shield) | +30% thrust, limiter off, worse reliability | Earn by rank; paint unlock is the reward | Char's Zaku/Gelgoog, Zaku Amazing, Schwalbe Graze, Custom Flag, Johnny Ridden |
| **T3 Prototype (hero)** | Twin eyes, crest, tricolor, exposed hi-tech (glowing frame, condensers) | 120–150%; expensive, unique parts | Story-granted; one per arc | RX-78, Exia, Strike, Barbatos 1st form, Aerial |
| **T3b Late prototype / rebuilt** | Same frame with bolted-on salvage or a new pack; silhouette grows | Adds a gimmick (pack, drones, transform) | Mid-arc upgrade | Barbatos 4th/6th, Aile/Sword/Launcher, Build Strike → Star Build Strike, Zeta → Hyper Mega |
| **T4 Mass-produced derivative** | Hero body with visor, cheaper | 90% of T3 | Unlocked after T3 is proven; equips the whole squad | GM (from RX-78), Leo (from Tallgeese), GN-X, Dagger (from Strike), Jegan |
| **T5 Super prototype / overdrive** | Frame that opens/glows/transforms; wings of light; twin drive | 200% for a timer, then crash | Endgame or "awakening" | Unicorn Destroy, 00 Raiser Trans-Am, V2 Wings, God Gundam Hyper Mode, Calibarn |
| **T6 Relic / forbidden** | Retro-futurist smooth, or ancient skeletal, or colossal MA | Story-gated one-use power | Final act | Turn A, Bael, Big Zam, Devil Gundam, Moonlight Butterfly |
| **Rival mirror** | The player's T3/T5 in black, red, or gold with a different crest (lion mane, horns) | Equal stats, opposite gimmick | Boss; later unlockable | Banshee vs Unicorn, Epyon vs Wing Zero, Master vs God, Aegis vs Strike, Vidar vs Barbatos, Schwarzette vs Aerial |

Recurring rules of thumb pulled from the whole catalog:
- **Head = tier.** Visor = grunt; mono-eye = enemy grunt; twin eyes + crest = hero; single horn/no face = "sealed" state; horn splits into crest = awakening.
- **Color = allegiance and rank**, not stats: tricolor hero; olive/grey grunt; one bright ace color; black/gold rival; white/gold "king" unit; gold coating = anti-beam gimmick.
- **Asymmetry sells specialization**: one spiked shoulder, one giant claw, a stacked wing of drones on one side, one arm that is a gun.
- **Every transformable has a "what the mode is for"**: re-entry glider, loiter disc, jet interceptor, manta net-pack, grabbing claw, three-way split, hover slide, bike mount, centaur charge, siege crouch. Give each transform a *job*, not just a shape.
- **Gimmicks come with a bill**: timers (NT-D, Trans-Am, Big Zam heat), sanity (Zero), body (Alaya-Vijnana, Permet), ammo (magnum, buster rifle, nuke), one-time cloaks (ABC), battery (Phase Shift).
- **Beam vs ballistic is a world rule**, not a stat: pick which one your setting makes "obsolete" (IBO: beams; SEED PS: bullets; UC I-field: beams for bosses) and let armor systems flip it.
- **Salvage and kitbash is a progression loop** (IBO forms, Build Fighters): a hero frame that visibly accumulates enemy parts reads as a story.
- **Packs are the cheap way to give one hero many silhouettes** (Striker, Silhouette, G-Self backpacks, V2 Assault/Buster, Hazel test parts, 0 Raiser docking).
- **Drones need a gate**: a pilot "sense" stat, a link score, an AI co-pilot (Haro), or a wire — and a counter (antidote, jamming, hijack).
